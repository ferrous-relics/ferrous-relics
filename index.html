Lot 03 — The Collection

What is in the boxes

Every FERO token begins life as a sealed box. Its contents were fixed before the window opened: the registrar committed a hash of the reveal seed to the contract in advance, so the assignment of relics to boxes was settled before a single box was claimed — and could not be rearranged afterwards. Nobody, including the registrar, can say which box holds what until the reveal.

When the window closes, the seed is published, the contract checks it against the committed hash, and all 3,333 boxes open simultaneously. From that moment each relic's tier and traits — background, frame, motif, core, ornament — are computed by the contract itself, permanently, from the seed and the box's number.

Boxes that nobody claimed or bought are not destroyed. After the reveal they pass to the treasury, which offers them through the exchange desk on this page.

Lot 05 — Procedure

How a box is acquired

  1. Post once on X. A post is the admission ticket for a free box; it is checked by the registrar before any free claim is signed. Post from the claim forms in Lot 02 — they compose the notice with this catalogue's address and your personal file reference.
  2. Claim one free box. Two free routes run side by side: one box for holders of a Binance Agent ID, one box for X posters. A wallet may use both routes — one post per claim — for a maximum of two free boxes.
  3. Buy more, with no further posting. Paid boxes ask nothing but the price: 0.001 BNB each, up to 100 per transaction, no limit per wallet. What is free is paid for with a post; what is paid for is paid for with BNB.

Lot 06 — The Exchange Desk

  
Trade boxes and relics

  
The registry keeps its own exchange desk, a separate contract with fixed terms: listings are at whatever price the seller names, in BNB, and they do not expire. The desk takes a 2.5% fee from the seller's proceeds, paid to the treasury; the seller receives the remaining 97.5%. Sealed boxes may be traded before the reveal — a buyer takes the box exactly as the seller held it, unopened.

  
The board below is kept by this page alone: every offer is read directly from the desk's records on-chain and checked against its book before it is shown — there is no indexer and no other board. If a sale's proceeds cannot be delivered to a seller's wallet directly, they wait at the desk as a pending withdrawal the seller can collect.
